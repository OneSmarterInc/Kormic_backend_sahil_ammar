rehearsed; student and university test sessions ready; authorized department inbox
available; one fresh unused SSGMCE invitation ready. Do not record secrets.

**Opening:** show production URL and introduce the demo student and Franklin.
Say: “Answers must cite evidence actually read. Missing evidence becomes a tracked
department query; the agent must not guess.”

**Ten questions, in order:** append to each: “Cite the official Franklin page you
actually read. If the evidence cannot confirm the answer, raise a query with the
appropriate department and tell me what is pending.” Show the citation/outcome.
